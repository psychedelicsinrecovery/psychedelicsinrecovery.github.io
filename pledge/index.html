A gratitude pledge

If the fellowship has meant something to you, you can say so in writing, by signing a short pledge with a crypto wallet. It's a thank-you, not a gift: no money is involved, nothing is sent anywhere, and for now nothing is recorded. PIR® is self-supporting through its own members, in keeping with the 7th Tradition, and that doesn't change here.
